Icon Background Color

The three overlapping lime-green keys on different background colors. Which feels most like a credential manager?

A. Dark Green (#052E16)

Deep forest green from your dark theme palette. High contrast with lime keys. Feels secure, premium. Matches your dark mode surface color.

B. Near Black (#1C1917)

Stone 900 — almost black. Maximum contrast. Feels bold and modern. Neutral, doesn't tie to any theme color.

C. White (#FFFFFF)

Clean white background. Lighter, friendlier feel. Keys pop with their green. Works well on dark OS surfaces (dark mode dock, dark taskbar).

Note: The extension toolbar icon (16px) will use just the keys without background for legibility at small sizes.
