Icon Style: Three Keys Side by Side

Which visual style fits KeyKeyKey best? These are conceptual directions — the final icon will be refined.

A. Flat Minimal

Clean flat icons, single color (lime green #A3E635). Uniform stroke weight, no gradients or shadows. Modern and app-icon friendly.

B. Bold Filled (Gradient Tones)

Solid filled keys with tonal variation (light→medium→dark lime). Center key is taller for visual hierarchy. More icon-like and recognizable at small sizes.

C. Overlapping / Layered

Three keys slightly overlapping with the center key in front and taller. Gives depth and a "bundle" feeling. Outlined with tonal greens for dimension.

Context

The icon needs to work at all sizes: 16x16 (extension toolbar), 29x29 (iOS small), up to 512x512 (App Store). Simpler designs scale better to small sizes. The current brand color is lime green (#A3E635).
